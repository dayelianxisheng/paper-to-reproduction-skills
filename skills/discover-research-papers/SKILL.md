# Discover Research Papers

## Workflow

1. Clarify the topic, date range, task, and must-have keywords.
2. Search current official proceedings, arXiv, publisher pages, and author
   project pages. Expand strong seed papers through references and related work.
3. Deduplicate by title, DOI, and arXiv ID. Distinguish formal publication year
   from first preprint year.
4. Rank by relevance, technical contribution, evidence quality, and availability
   of official code or weights.
5. Return a compact table with title, year/venue, relevance, official paper/PDF
   link, and any verified official project link.

Prefer primary sources and verify current claims online. Do not download PDFs;
hand the selected list to `$download-research-papers`.
